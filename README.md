# EventWillow

EventWillow brings event discovery and organizing together. Find something happening nearby, publish events for your community, and keep attendees up to date from one place.

## What you can do

- Discover events through searchable calendars and event pages.
- Publish one-time or recurring events and share them with your audience.
- Offer free registration or sell tickets, with attendee lists and check-in tools.
- Keep people informed with updates, reminders, and newsletters.
- Share calendars on your website and let visitors follow the events they care about.
- Coordinate venues, performers, appointments, and community event submissions.

## Explore

Visit [EventWillow](https://eventwillow.com) to explore events and schedules. For help using the platform, see the [guides](https://eventwillow/docs).
